  <h2>Summary</h2>
  <p>Alvagante does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, profiling cookies, login cookies, or newsletter tracking cookies.</p>

  <h2>Essential Hosting and Security Technologies</h2>
  <p>The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and may be served through Cloudflare. These providers may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies for security, abuse prevention, load balancing, or delivery of the website. These technologies are controlled by the infrastructure providers and are not used by this site for advertising or profiling.</p>

  <h2>Third-Party Resources</h2>
  <p>The links directory loads favicon images from Google's favicon service at <code>www.google.com/s2/favicons</code>. This is used only to display recognizable icons for curated links. Google may receive technical request information when those images are loaded. Generated news items may load images from the original news source if a feed provides an image URL.</p>

  <h2>Local Development Only</h2>
  <p>When the site is served locally through Docker, Jekyll LiveReload may run on port <code>35729</code>. This is a development-only feature and is not part of the public production site.</p>

  <h2>Cookie Banner</h2>
  <p>No cookie banner is shown because this public site does not intentionally set non-essential cookies. If analytics, embedded media, advertising, or other non-essential tracking is added later, this policy and the consent flow should be updated before deployment.</p>

  <h2>Managing Cookies</h2>
  <p>You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary hosting or security cookies may affect site delivery.</p>
